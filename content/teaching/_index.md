I have taught at two institutions: as instructor of record for Intermediate Macroeconomics at San José State University (Fall 2026) and UC Santa Cruz (Summer 2025), and as a teaching assistant for 18 course-quarters at UCSC spanning econometrics, data science, macroeconomics, economic history, and policy writing. My courses are structured like an empirical paper: each unit starts from a question and the evidence, then builds the model needed to answer it.
